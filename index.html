      About MarketMate

      MarketMate is an AI-powered marketing assistant designed for small businesses. It turns business data into practical marketing ideas, from ready-to-use social media captions and promotional campaigns to stock-aware restock and out-of-stock updates. Our goal is to help small businesses make smarter marketing decisions with less effort.
